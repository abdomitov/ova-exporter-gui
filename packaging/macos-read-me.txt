OVA Exporter GUI for macOS (Intel)

1. Drag "OVA Exporter GUI" onto the Applications folder.
2. Open Applications and double-click the app.
3. The app is not notarized by Apple yet, so macOS blocks the first launch:
   - macOS 15 Sequoia and later: click Done, open System Settings, Privacy & Security,
     scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to OVA Exporter GUI, then confirm.
   - macOS 14 and earlier: Control-click the app, choose Open, then click Open.
   Compare the disk image's SHA-256 with the .sha256 file on the release page first.
4. Install VMware OVF Tool for macOS from Broadcom separately. The app finds it in
   /Applications/VMware OVF Tool, or click "Locate ovftool" in Settings.

Settings are encrypted with a key kept in your login Keychain. Passwords are never saved.
